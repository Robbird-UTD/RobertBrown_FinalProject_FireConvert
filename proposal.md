
# Fire Convert

## Repository
https://github.com/Robbird-UTD/RobertBrown_FinalProject_FireConvert

## Description
- **What'll it do?:** Converts Fire into black and white with only Black or White using tools.
- **Relevance?:** I will likely use this in the future for my art style. Fire looks cool, but I need to have an easy way to make fire black and white and high contrast quickly for animated fires.

## Features
- *Feature 1*
	- Will convert an image into black and white and high contrast and maybe 100% transparancy as a 4th color.
- *Feature 2*
	- Will grab the frames of a video, copy, then apply the conversion, then back to a video and give me the individual frames in a folder too. 
- *Feature N*
	- More conversion features that I can choose from using PyGame.
